---
title: Privacy Policy for Nations Rising
---

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

Nations Rising ("the app") is a single-player world strategy game made by Lodestar Forge ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

## Summary

- Your game, saves and settings stay on your device.
- The app has **no** advertising, **no** analytics and **no** accounts.
- If you buy something in the app, a small amount of purchase information is processed so the purchase can be verified and restored. That is the only data that leaves your device.
- We do not sell your data or use it for tracking.

## Data stored on your device

The app saves your game worlds, save slots, records, achievements and settings in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## In-app purchases

The game can be played without paying. Optional purchases (the Full Edition, extra scenarios and cosmetics) are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

To check and restore purchases, the app uses **RevenueCat** (revenuecat.com), a purchase-verification service. When the app starts, and when you make or restore a purchase, the following are sent to RevenueCat over an encrypted connection:

- a random, app-generated user identifier (not linked to your name or store account), and
- your purchase history for this app (which items were bought, and when).

This data is used only to unlock what you bought, and is not used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. On Android it uses the standard `INTERNET` permission, which is needed only for the purchase verification described above.

## Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It contains no chat and no user-generated content shared with others. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. To ask for your purchase records to be deleted from RevenueCat, email us at the address below. Note that deleted purchase records may mean purchases cannot be restored later.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests about your data, can be sent to: **support@caldervalley.tech**
